🚀 Getting Started (3 Easy Steps)
1
Open the File & Enter Your Code
Double-click WeddingPlannerPro_WebApp.html — it opens in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. Enter your unlock code BRIDE2026 and click "Unlock My Planner". You'll only need to do this once — your browser remembers it forever.
2
Go to Settings First
Click Settings in the left sidebar. Enter your names, wedding date, venue, and total budget. Hit Save Changes — your details will update across every tab instantly, including the live countdown.
3
Pick Your Color Theme
Still in Settings, scroll down to Color Theme. Choose from 8 palettes — Dusty Blue, Sage Green, Blush Pink, Lavender, Neutral Taupe, Rose Gold, Classic Navy, or Emerald. Your whole dashboard recolors instantly and saves automatically.
💾
Your data saves automatically in your browser's local storage every time you make a change. Always keep the index.html file in a safe place (iCloud, Google Drive, or Desktop) as a backup. Use the "Export Full PDF" button in the top bar to export all 18 tabs as a complete, branded PDF — then choose Save as PDF in the print dialog that appears.

💡 Pro Tips
🎨
Match your theme: Try all 8 color palettes in Settings — each one completely recolors your entire dashboard in real time.
🪑
Seating chart: Drag and drop guest cards between tables. Click "+ Add Table" to create new tables. Click ✕ on a table to remove it and move guests back to Unassigned.
💰
Expense status: The Paid/Pending badge updates automatically — once your Actual amount equals or exceeds the Budgeted amount, it flips to Paid.
📄
Export your full planner as PDF: Click "Export Full PDF" in the top bar. A print dialog will open — set the Destination to Save as PDF, then click Save. All 18 tabs export as one complete, branded document.
🗺️
Venue map: In Settings, type your venue address and wait a moment — a live Google Map will appear below automatically.
📱
Works on any device: Open index.html on your phone or tablet browser for a responsive mobile experience.
🔄
Fresh start: Click "Reset" in the top bar to clear all data and start from scratch. Your color theme is preserved.
☁️
Sync across devices: Store index.html in iCloud Drive or Google Drive and open it on any device — data saves per-browser via localStorage.
